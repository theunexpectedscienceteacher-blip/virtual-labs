Rescue Run Virtual Engineering Lab

Replacement major-grade pathway: research, build a gravity-powered rescue vehicle, stay within budget, run trials, revise the design, graph motion data, and complete a final engineering write-up.

Major Grade Expectations

This virtual assignment replaces the full build project only when a student cannot participate in the classroom build. Students still complete the engineering cycle and must support final claims with data.
Your work saves automatically on this device and browser. If you close the tab, come back on the same computer to keep going.

Challenge

A storm has damaged the road to a remote community. You must design a gravity-powered vehicle that carries one rescue package toward a target 200 cm east of the starting line.

Success Criteria

  • Stay at or under the $200 budget.
  • Carry the package during the trial.
  • Travel at least 200 cm toward the target.
  • Collect three official trials.
  • Calculate speed and velocity correctly.
  • Use data to justify a redesign.

Design Plan

Test Your Vehicle

Your vehicle is released from the top of a 30 cm ramp. Timing starts when it crosses the start line at the bottom of the ramp. Timing gates record the time at every 50 cm. The target is 200 cm east of the start line.

Practice Run lets you try your design as many times as you want (not recorded). Official Trial records your data. You get 3 official trials for each design.
