Element: only ONE kind of atom (one symbol), even if the atoms are joined in pairs like O₂. Compound: TWO OR MORE different elements chemically joined (two or more symbols). Click a card, then click a bin.

🔥 Mission 3: Physical or Chemical Change?

Left12
Correct0

Physical change: same substance, new size, shape, or state (melting, freezing, cutting, dissolving). Chemical change: a NEW substance forms. Look for evidence: gas bubbles, color change, temperature change, light, a new smell, or a precipitate (a solid forming in a liquid).
