📈 Graph Lab: Distance-Time Graphs

Move the runner to match a graph, build graphs from stories, and read graphs like a detective.

7.7C Distance-time graphs7.7A Average speed

🏃 Station 1: Motion Matcher

Press Start, then change the runner's speed so the teal line follows the gold dashed target. A steeper line means faster, a flat line means stopped, and a line going down means heading back toward the start. You can also press keys 1–5.

✏️ Station 2: Graph Builder

Click on the graph to place a point at 5 s, 10 s, 15 s, and 20 s. The point at 0 s is already placed. Click again in the same column to move a point. Points snap to the nearest 5 m.
