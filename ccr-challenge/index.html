How to tell them apart

♨️ Conduction
Energy moves by direct contact. Look for two things touching.
💨 Convection
Energy is carried by a moving fluid (liquid or gas). Look for currents: warm rises, cool sinks.
☀️ Radiation
Energy travels as electromagnetic waves. No contact needed. It can even cross empty space.

Remember: thermal energy always flows from hotter to colder.
